    Display settings: household, source or region¶

    Status: spec (owner agreed 2026-10-30). Frontend only. Every display setting gets exactly one home, picked by what it describes:

    It describes… Home Examples
    the household / locale Content, global week starts on, weekend days, time format, holidays
    one source that source's row (Content) colour, show times, address, refresh
    how one region looks that region, in Pages & regions (and on-dashboard controls) calendar view, week numbers, events per day, photo fit

    No setting lives in two places. Region settings have no global default layer.

    1. Calendar¶

    • Content › Calendar basics (renamed from Calendar display) keeps: Week starts on, Weekend days, Time format, Highlight holidays.
    • Content › Calendars gets a Where calendars show list (§5) instead of a bare pointer.
    • Removed from Content: Show week numbers, Events per day.
    • Region inspector, calendar section, after Weeks ahead:
    • Week numbers: ToggleSwitch, writes view.weekNumbers (already read by CalendarView.vue).
    • Events per day: SelectPill Auto · 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 6 · 8, writes view.maxVisibleEvents (null = Auto). Auto fits as many event rows as the day cell's measured height allows (row height from the rendered chip; at least 1), and shows "+N more" for the rest.
    • On the dashboard: the calendar's existing view controls (calendar-header__controls) get a ⋯ with Week numbers on/off. Events per day stays in the editor (it's set once, not toggled).

    Upgrade: on first load after the change, every calendar region without its own view.weekNumbers / view.maxVisibleEvents gets today's global showWeekNumbers / maxVisibleEvents. Do it once (flag displaySettingsMigrated: true in config), then stop reading the global keys.

    2. Photos¶

    2.1 Smart fit isn't built yet¶

    PhotoSlideshow.vue "smart" never measures the region. It assumes every photo region is a ~30% side panel (containerAspect hardcoded to 0.59 landscape / 1.7 portrait). In a full-width region, or in photo-frame fullscreen on a landscape screen (aspect ~1.78), nearly every photo compares as "wider than 0.59" and gets cover. A portrait photo then shows as a cropped band. Also, Fill and Crop both render object-fit: cover, so they are the same option.

    2.2 Smart, for real¶

    • Measure the photo area with a ResizeObserver: containerAspect = width / height, updated on resize and when entering or leaving fullscreen / photo frame.
    • Per photo, with r = imageAspect / containerAspect:
    • 0.8 ≤ r ≤ 1.25 → fill (cover): at most ~20% is cropped;
    • otherwise → whole photo (contain) over a blurred backdrop: the same image, object-fit: cover, filter: blur(28px) brightness(.55), scaled 1.1 to hide blur edges, aria-hidden. No black bars.
    • image.width/height missing → load the image and use its naturalWidth/naturalHeight. Until then, show it contain.
    • Reduced data: the backdrop reuses the loaded image (no second request).

    2.3 Where Fit lives¶

    • Fit photos moves to the region inspector, photos section: SelectPill Smart · Whole photo · Fill · Centre (Crop removed; it was Fill). It writes view.fit; missing = Smart. Whole photo also gets the blurred backdrop.
    • Photo frame (fullscreen when idle) always uses Smart: it's a different shape from any region.
    • Stays in Content › Photos (they're about the collection's rhythm, and photo frame uses them too): Show each photo for, Shuffle photos, Photo frame when idle, Start after.
    • Upgrade: copy the global imageDisplayMode into each photos region's view.fit once (same flag). crop → fill.

    3. Content stays the hub (§5 applies to every kind)¶

    Content loses three rows (week numbers, events per day, photo fit) and gains a view of where each kind shows. Content answers "what Calvin shows, and where"; the editor answers "how this region looks". So Content isn't downgraded: it becomes the place you start, and every look setting is one tap away from it.

    4. Web pages & apps, and the clock bar¶

    No moves. When tapped is already per region. Address, zoom and refresh are per source. The clock bar's show and position are already per page, and its look stays global.

    5. "Where it shows" in each Content section¶

    Under the source list of Calendars, Photos and Web pages & apps, a compact list headed Where {kind} show (--fs-sm, 600): - One line per region of that kind, across all pages: {page} · {region label}, then a summary of its look in --ink-2: - Calendars: Month · week numbers · Auto events / Agenda, 7 days; - Photos: Smart fit; - Web pages & apps: the source name. It also lists which sources the region shows when it doesn't show all of them: Family, School. - Each line is a button (≥ 44px) that opens Pages & regions on that page with that region selected. - None: Not on any page yet. + Put it on a page (the place mode from the content & plugins spec §3.6). - For Web pages & apps, this replaces nothing: the per-row placement line (content & plugins §3.6) stays, and this list is the region view of the same thing.

    6. Tests (vitest)¶

    • Content › Calendar basics has no week-number or events-per-day rows.
    • Where it shows: one line per region of the kind with its look summary and source filter; a tap opens the editor with that region selected; none → Put it on a page.
    • The region inspector writes view.weekNumbers and view.maxVisibleEvents. Auto fits the rows to the cell height (mock height → count) and shows "+N more".
    • The on-dashboard ⋯ toggles week numbers for that region only.
    • Migration copies the globals into regions without their own values, runs once (flag), and maps crop → fill.
    • Smart fit:
    • a 16:9 container with a 3:2 photo → fill;
    • a 16:9 container with a 2:3 photo → contain plus backdrop;
    • a 9:16 container with a 16:9 photo → contain plus backdrop;
    • a resize recomputes it;
    • missing dimensions fall back to naturalWidth/Height.
    • Fit options: no Crop; Whole photo renders the backdrop; photo frame uses Smart whatever the region's fit is.
